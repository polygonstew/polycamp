You can build the most beautiful model in the world and make it look like a clay prototype with bad lighting. Photographers and
cinematographers spend more of their lives on light than on anything else, and a 3D render is photography with a better budget.

This world is about **where the light comes from, what colour it is, and how soft its shadows are**, then about the camera
that observes it, and finally about **getting a clean render out of a CPU** without waiting until next week. It ends with the
first proper still of your diorama at dusk.
