Animation in Blender is one idea repeated in different outfits: **tell Blender what a number should be at two moments, and it fills in the rest.** Position, rotation, a light's power, a
material's colour, a camera's focus, how open a door is. Anything with a number can be keyframed.

This world covers the timeline and keyframes, the **Graph Editor** where you shape *how* things move between keys (where polish lives), animating a **camera**, a light that **flickers** (the lantern!),
**shape keys** and a simple **armature**, and then the part nobody warns you about: rendering many frames on a CPU.

The boss animates the diorama: a slow orbit around the cabin while the lantern flickers, rendered to a video.
