Welcome to Polycamp. You are not here to watch videos. You are here to **do things in Blender**, and the
link will check your work live, like a coding bootcamp but for polygons.

This world is the pre-flight check: connect Blender to this app, set it up so your CPU-only machine stays
happy, and walk through the interface once, properly. By the boss you will have built a totem pole out
of nothing but primitives, which is a surprisingly good test of whether the basics stuck.

You already know how to move around and what the axes are, so we go fast. We slow down for the things
people usually skip: preferences, snapping, naming, and saving.
