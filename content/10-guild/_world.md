Nothing new is taught here. This is the exam, and the portfolio piece.

You have a cabin, a lantern, rocks, pines, a fence, a hand-made sky, textures from the Lab, lights, a camera and an orbit with a flickering flame. The last world is for finishing: tying the pieces into one scene that is yours,
polishing it, and producing the final still and the final animation.

You can use the scene you built across the earlier worlds (open your own file, and press **Claim** where the app asks) or let **Set up scene** build the course's reference version so you can concentrate on the finishing touches. If you built your own, use it.
It will be better for it.
