Everything you see in a 3D model is points joined by lines joined into flat surfaces. Modelling is
deciding where those points go. This world is the one with the most muscle memory in it: **Edit Mode**,
**extrude**, **inset**, **loop cut**, **bevel**, and the unglamorous rules about topology that keep a
mesh from falling apart later.

Nothing here needs artistic talent. It needs repetition. The Hotkey Dojo is a good companion for this
world, ten minutes at a time.

The boss is a mug. Round body, hollow inside, handle on the side. It sounds simple, and then you meet
your first handle.
