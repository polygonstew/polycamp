A scene is made of **places and props**, and both start the same way: rough shapes, placed carefully, then
refined. This world is about *environment modelling*: how big things are, how to build architecture fast,
how to scatter a dozen similar objects without copy-paste misery, and how to make rocks and ground that
do not look like they were extruded by a ruler.

Everything you make here goes into the final diorama: the **Lantern Cabin at Dusk**. A round slice of
land, a log cabin with a glowing window, a lantern by the door, pines, rocks, a fence. By the boss you
will have all of it standing in one scene, grey and uncoloured, and it will already look like a place.
