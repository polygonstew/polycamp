# Polycamp

A boot camp for Blender. You do the work in Blender, a small add-on reports your scene to a local web app,
and the app checks each task as you go. Built for two monitors (it works on one), CPU-only machines, Linux and Windows.

    python3 server/polycamp.py        # http://<this-pc>:8570 on your LAN

Then open the app, press **Link Blender**, download `polycamp_link.zip` and install it in Blender 3.3, 4.x or 5.x.

Includes: a lesson campaign, a Hotkey Dojo, a Texture Lab (procedural or ComfyUI, PBR maps, send to Blender or Godot/Kiln),
a UV Lab, and a Trophy Room for your renders. See `docs/HANDOFF.md` for the current state and what is unfinished.
